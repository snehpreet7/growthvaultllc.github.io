ScanLean AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Information We Collect

ScanLean AI collects photos you submit for body fat analysis, basic profile information (age, gender, height, weight), and subscription information.

How We Use Your Information

Photos are used solely for AI body fat analysis and are not stored on our servers. Your profile data is used to generate personalized fitness plans.

Data Storage

Photos submitted for analysis are processed in real-time and immediately deleted. We do not store your body photos.

Third Party AI Service

ScanLean AI uses Anthropic Claude AI to analyze your body photos for body fat estimation. When you scan your body, your photo is transmitted to Anthropic's servers for processing. Photos are processed instantly and immediately deleted after analysis. No photos are stored permanently. Anthropic's privacy policy can be found at anthropic.com/privacy.

Subscription Management

We use RevenueCat for subscription management and processing. RevenueCat's privacy policy can be found at revenuecat.com/privacy.
